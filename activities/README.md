# Activities — Effective Project Management for Small Projects

**Course code:** TGS-2020505545  
**Version:** v18.0 · 19 August 2026  

All seven activities use one connected Singapore SME project. Each folder is self-contained with the detailed procedure, evidence worksheet, editable Office tools and any supplied dataset.

## Running case

**HarbourHub Fresh — Click & Collect Pilot** — A 42-person Singapore specialty grocer will introduce online ordering and two-hour click-and-collect at three outlets. The five-month pilot has a S$180,000 cap, a core team of seven, two external suppliers, and limited dependencies. It is important to operations but does not change the company's overall strategy, making it a genuine small project.

**Success target:** Launch at all three outlets by 30 January 2027; process at least 120 pilot orders per week; keep pick accuracy at or above 98%; achieve customer satisfaction of at least 4.2/5; and remain within the approved S$180,000 budget.

## Evidence chain

```text
1 Charter and value case
  ↓
2 Integrated scope-schedule-cost baseline
  ↓
3 Quality diagnosis and improvement action
  ↓
4 Risk register and funded responses
  ↓
5 Stakeholder map, RACI and engagement plan
  ↓
6 Capacity plan and resource decision
  ↓
7 Status, change decision, corrective action and closure
```

The PowerPoint deliberately does not repeat the procedures. Follow the Learner Guide or each activity README, then preserve the completed editable tools as evidence.
